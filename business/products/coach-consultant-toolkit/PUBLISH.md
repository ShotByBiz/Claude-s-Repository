## Make the sellable file (2 min)
1. Open `the-client-engine.html` in any browser.
2. **Print → Save as PDF.** That PDF is what buyers download.
   *(Optional: also keep the PDF in this folder.)*

## List it on Gumroad (10 min) — primary channel
1. Go to **gumroad.com**, sign in (or Join — free) with **shotbybizllc@gmail.com**.
2. **Products → New product → Digital product.**
3. Name, description, tags, price ($29): copy them from `listing-kit.md`
   (the GUMROAD section — all written for you).
4. Upload the **PDF** as the content, and **`cover.svg`** (or a PNG export) as the cover.
5. Under Settings, finish **payout** (connect bank / PayPal) so money can reach you.
6. **Publish.** Copy the product link.

## Drive traffic (3 min to start)
1. Open `launch-posts.md`.
2. Post **Post 1** to LinkedIn/X, put your Gumroad link in the first reply and in
   your bio. Engage every comment.
3. Space out posts 2–4 over the next two weeks.
