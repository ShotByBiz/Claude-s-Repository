Why most coaches waste AI — and how this fixes it

Most "prompt packs" hand you a list and leave. You paste a prompt, get generic output, and quietly stop using it. The problem was never the prompts — it's that a prompt with no strategy around it produces advice you can't sell with.

The Client Engine is built around the six workflows that actually move money in a coaching or consulting business — not "content ideas," but the sequence that turns a stranger into a paying, renewing client. Each prompt is written to be filled in with your real offer and voice, each section tells you why it works, and you get tested example outputs so you know what "good" looks like before you send anything.

How to get the most from it: work the six workflows in order for one client, top to bottom. Fill every {{bracket}} with your real details — the more specific your input, the less generic the output. Star (★) marks the biggest time-savers.
The six workflows
  1. Attract — pull in ideal clients
  2. Convert — discovery calls & proposals
  3. Offer — package & price it
  4. Onboard — a kickoff they rave about
  5. Deliver — sessions that get results
  6. Retain & Refer — renewals, testimonials, referrals
Workflow 1

Attract — pull in ideal clients

Goal: stop chasing. Produce content and lead magnets that make the right people raise their hand. Why it works: AI is strongest when it starts from a sharp picture of one person's pain — so every prompt here forces you to name the exact client and problem first.

★ Ideal-client pain map
Act as a positioning strategist. My coaching niche is {{niche}} and my ideal client is {{who they are}}. List the top 7 problems they lie awake worrying about, the exact words they'd use to describe each (not jargon), and the one outcome they'd pay almost anything for. Rank by how urgently they'd act.
★ 30-day content calendar
You are my content strategist. Using this pain list — {{paste pains}} — build a 30-day content calendar (3 posts/week) for {{platform}}. For each: a hook line, the core idea, and the single action I want the reader to take. Vary formats (story, myth-bust, quick win, case snapshot).
Scroll-stopping hooks
Write 15 first-line hooks for a post about {{topic}} aimed at {{ideal client}}. Make them specific and curiosity-driven, no clickbait. Avoid "In today's world" and any cliché opener.
Lead magnet that pre-sells
Design a free lead magnet for {{ideal client}} that solves one painful micro-problem in under 10 minutes and naturally leads them to want my paid help with {{bigger problem}}. Give me the title, the outline, and the one-sentence promise.
Signature framework namer
I help clients {{transformation}} using this process: {{your steps}}. Package it into a memorable named framework (3–5 steps, each a short verb-led phrase) I can teach and be known for. Give 5 name options.
DM opener that isn't creepy
Write 5 warm, non-salesy DM openers to start a real conversation with someone who engaged with my post about {{topic}}. The goal is a genuine reply, not a pitch. Keep each under 30 words.
Repurpose one idea 6 ways
Take this single idea — {{idea}} — and turn it into: a short-form video script, a carousel outline, an email, a LinkedIn post, a one-line tweet, and a lead-magnet section. Keep my voice: {{tone}}.
Workflow 2

Convert — discovery calls & proposals

Goal: turn interested people into paying clients without feeling "salesy." Why it works: conversion is a structure problem, not a charisma problem — these give you the questions, the framing, and the follow-up that make the yes feel obvious.

★ Discovery-call script
Build me a discovery-call script for {{ideal client}} seeking {{outcome}}. Structure: rapport → their current situation → the gap and its cost → the desired outcome → whether my {{offer}} fits → clear next step. Give the exact questions, and note where to shut up and listen.
★ Objection responses
A prospect said: "{{objection}}." Give me 3 honest, non-pushy ways to respond that acknowledge them, reframe the real issue, and move the decision forward. No manipulation.
Proposal from call notes
Turn these discovery-call notes — {{notes}} — into a one-page proposal: their goal in their words, what we'll do, the outcome, the investment ({{price}}), and a single clear next step. Confident, concise, no fluff.
The follow-up sequence
Write a 3-message follow-up sequence for a warm prospect who went quiet after a good call. Message 1 (value + soft nudge), 2 (address likely hesitation about {{concern}}), 3 (respectful last check). Keep each short and human.
Price-anchor explainer
Help me explain the value of my {{price}} offer to {{ideal client}} by anchoring it against the cost of not solving {{problem}}. Give me 3 framings I can say out loud naturally.
Qualify-out politely
Write a kind, professional message to decline working with a poor-fit prospect ({{why not a fit}}) while preserving the relationship and, where possible, pointing them to a better resource.
Workflow 3

Offer — package & price it

Goal: a clear, confident offer people understand in one read. Why it works: confused buyers don't buy; these prompts force one outcome, one path, one price.

★ Signature offer builder
Design my core coaching offer for {{ideal client}} who wants {{outcome}}. Give: a compelling name, the promised result and timeframe, what's included (sessions, support, materials), who it's NOT for, and a one-sentence pitch. Keep it to one transformation.
Three-tier ladder
Turn my offer into 3 tiers (entry, core, premium) with clear differences in access and outcome, priced around {{core price}}. Make the middle tier the obvious best value.
Offer one-pager copy
Write the sales one-pager for {{offer name}}: headline, the problem, the promise, what's inside, who it's for, proof placeholder, price, and CTA. Voice: {{tone}}.
Guarantee that de-risks
Propose 3 fair, honest guarantee options for my {{offer}} that lower the buyer's risk without exposing me to abuse. Explain the tradeoff of each.
Name the transformation
My clients go from {{before state}} to {{after state}}. Give me 10 ways to describe that transformation in the client's own language for use in titles and headlines.
Upsell / continuation path
After a client finishes {{offer}}, what's the natural next offer that keeps them progressing? Design it and write the one-line invitation I'd give them near the end of our engagement.
Entry (tripwire) offer
Design a low-risk entry offer (a paid intro session, audit, or mini-program) that lets a hesitant {{ideal client}} experience my work for {{low price}} and naturally leads into {{core offer}}. Give the name, what's included, and the one-line pitch.
Workflow 4

Onboard — a kickoff they rave about

Goal: the first 7 days set the tone for the whole engagement. Why it works: a smooth, confident onboarding reduces refunds and buyer's remorse and turns new clients into future testimonials.

★ Welcome + intake email
Write a warm welcome email for a new {{offer}} client. Confirm what happens next, set expectations, and include a short intake questionnaire (5–7 questions) that gets me everything I need to make session one great.
Kickoff session agenda
Build a 60-minute kickoff-session agenda for {{offer}}: goals, current reality, quick early win, roadmap, and how we'll measure progress. Include time blocks.
Client goal-setting worksheet
Create a goal-setting worksheet a new client fills before session one, covering their #1 outcome, obstacles, past attempts, and what success looks like in {{timeframe}}.
Expectations & boundaries note
Draft a friendly "how we'll work together" note covering communication channels, response times, rescheduling, and what's in/out of scope for {{offer}}. Warm but clear.
First quick-win plan
Based on this client's intake — {{intake}} — design one fast, meaningful win they can achieve in week one so they feel momentum immediately.
Onboarding checklist
Give me a step-by-step onboarding checklist (my side + client side) for the first 7 days of {{offer}}, so nothing falls through the cracks.
Workflow 5

Deliver — sessions that get results

Goal: show up prepared, coach deeper, and make progress visible. Why it works: AI is a tireless prep partner and thinking amplifier — it never replaces your judgment, it sharpens it.

★ Session prep from last notes
Here are my notes from the last session: {{notes}}. Prep me for the next one: what to follow up on, 3 powerful questions to ask, likely blockers, and a suggested focus. I'll adapt live.
Powerful coaching questions
Give me 12 open, non-leading coaching questions to help a client stuck on {{issue}} reach their own insight. Group by: clarify → challenge → commit.
Custom framework / exercise
Design a simple exercise or worksheet to help a client work through {{challenge}} between sessions. Make it doable in 20 minutes and easy to debrief next time.
Reframe a stuck belief
A client keeps saying "{{limiting belief}}." Give me 3 respectful reframes and a question for each that invites them to test the belief rather than defend it.
Progress recap for the client
Turn these session notes — {{notes}} — into a short, encouraging recap for the client: what we covered, their wins, their action steps, and one thing to notice this week.
Accountability check-in
Write a brief mid-week check-in message for a client working toward {{goal}} that holds them accountable warmly without nagging.
Resource curator
Suggest 5 high-quality resources (book, article, exercise, tool, habit) relevant to a client working on {{topic}}, with a one-line reason each is worth their time.
Workflow 6

Retain & Refer — renewals, testimonials, referrals

Goal: turn great outcomes into recurring revenue and a referral flywheel. Why it works: your happiest clients are your cheapest marketing — these prompts make asking natural and easy.

★ Renewal / continuation offer
My client is finishing {{offer}} and has made this progress: {{results}}. Write a warm, no-pressure message inviting them to continue with {{next offer}}, tied to the momentum they've built.
★ Testimonial request that gets a yes
Write a short message asking a happy client for a testimonial, with 4 guiding questions so they know exactly what to say (their before, the turning point, the result, who they'd recommend it to). Make it 2-minute-easy.
Turn a testimonial into a case study
Turn this raw testimonial — {{quote}} — into a short, believable case-study snippet (challenge → what we did → result) I can use in marketing. Keep their voice; don't exaggerate.
Referral ask
Write a natural referral request for a satisfied client: acknowledge their result, describe exactly who I'm a great fit for, and make introducing me effortless. No bribery-feeling incentives unless I add one.
Win-back a past client
Draft a genuine re-engagement message to a past client I haven't spoken to in {{time}}, referencing their goal of {{goal}} and offering a relevant next step. Warm, not desperate.
Off-boarding that leaves the door open
Write a closing message for a client wrapping up {{offer}}: celebrate their progress, summarize how far they've come, and leave a clear, welcoming path back.
Quarterly client-love audit
Give me a 6-point checklist to run quarterly to keep current clients feeling seen and supported so retention stays high.
Proof it works

3 tested example outputs

Here's what these prompts actually produce, so you know the quality before you use them. (Filled with a sample "confidence coach for new managers" niche.)

From: Ideal-client pain map

Top 3 lie-awake problems for new managers: (1) "I got promoted for my work, now I'm judged on my team's — and I don't know how to lead people I used to sit beside." (2) "I avoid the hard conversation until it explodes." (3) "I'm working nights to keep up because I can't delegate without feeling I've dumped on someone." Would-pay-anything outcome: "Walk into any 1:1 or tough conversation feeling calm and in control." Most urgent: #2 — it's actively costing them respect right now.

From: Discovery-call script (excerpt)

The gap & its cost: "You said you avoid the tough conversations until they blow up. What's that costing you — in your team's trust, and in your own stress?" (then stop and listen) … Next step: "Based on what you've shared, the 8-week Confident Manager track is built for exactly this. Want me to walk you through how it'd work for your situation?"

From: Testimonial request

"Hi Sam — I'd love to capture what you've built these past weeks. Could you answer these 4 quick questions? (1) What was hardest before we started? (2) What shifted? (3) What's different now for your team? (4) Who would you tell to do this? Two minutes, and it means the world."
